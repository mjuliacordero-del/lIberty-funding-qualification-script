Opening and discovery

#Verbatim / instructionReason / purpose

2WHY FUNDING: "So what is the biggest struggle in your business that has you reaching out for funding?"Open discovery: identify the business problem and motivation
3[REFLECT BACK YOUR UNDERSTANDING / SUMMARY]Demonstrate listening and confirm the problem before moving on
4HOW MUCH: "How much funding are you looking to acquire?"Qualify the requested funding amount

5AGE OF BUSINESS: "How long have you had this business registered?"Qualify business age

Education and qualification

#Verbatim / instructionReason / purpose
6[EDUCATE / CATEGORIZE OUR LANE]

"So this is a no-documentation process for this level of funding. We won't need proof of revenue or anything, but we do look at your personal credit..."		Explain the program category and set expectations about what is evaluated
7OVER $10K UNSECURED / CREDIT HISTORY: "What kind of personal credit cards do you have?"Assess available personal credit history and borrowing capacity
8IDEAL TIMELINE: "Do you have a deadline for this funding?"Identify urgency and compare it with a realistic process timeline
9REALISTIC TIMELINE: "Given what I hear so far, it looks like a realistic timeline to get to funding is likely [REALISTIC TIMELINE]"Set a realistic timeline when the available information supports it
[BRANCH A: ALL CRITERIA MET]: the next 6 weeks
[BRANCH B: MOST CRITERIA MET]: the next 3-4 months
[BRANCH B2: FEW CRITERIA MET]: 6-7 months
12[REALISTIC TIMELINE CHECK]: Does that work for you?Confirm acceptance of the timeline
13DEROGATORIES / LATE PAYS, ETC.: "Do you know on your credit — is there any other, I have to ask everybody this — any late pays, any derogatories, anything else?"Surface credit problems that could affect qualification
14INQUIRIES: "And roughly, do you know how many inquiries you have on your credit?"Reveal additional credit-file data and possible obstacles
15OTHER FUNDING ATTEMPTS: "What else have you tried for the funding?"Learn what the prospect has already attempted

Trust and funding-level confirmation

#Verbatim / instructionReason / purpose
16[EDUCATE / BUILD TRUST]

"And this is something we hear all the time — the funding space is saturated... Because of that, it's going to be very important for us to build trust."		Acknowledge market skepticism and explain why trust matters
17[TRUST PRE-OBJECTION HANDLING]

"Let's suppose you felt like we were the perfect fit for your larger vision, everything was also a match on your end, so we invited you into the program, what would you want to know about us, to feel comfortable investing in yourself through our program?"		Invite concerns before presenting the next step; surface the prospect's buying criteria
19[FUNDING LEVEL, CHECK]: Would you be able to use that effectively?Confirm the prospect can deploy a larger amount productively

Timeline reinforcement and investment capacity

#Verbatim / instructionReason / purpose
20[REINFORCE REALISTIC TIMELINE]

"Awesome, well [PROSPECT] — again, like I said, this is a real potential opportunity here, and [TIMELINE]."		Reinforce that the opportunity is real while keeping the timeline visible
21[BRANCH A: ALL CRITERIA MET]: that could potentially happen in the next 6 weeksUse the shortest timeline only when all criteria are met
22[BRANCH A.2: MOST CRITERIA MET]: that could potentially happen in the next 3-4 monthsUse a middle timeline when most criteria are met
23[BRANCH B: FEW CRITERIA MET]: but it is a long-term strategy.Set the long-term expectation when few criteria are met
24[LIQUIDITY, OPEN LOOP]

"One thing I do want to let you know is that this isn't something where — it's something that happens for free, right?"		Introduce the investment expectation without presenting it as a surprise later
25LIQUIDITY CONFIRMATION — EMPATHY FIRST BEFORE INVESTMENT: "I know .... [PROSPECT'S SPECIFIC CIRCUMSTANCE] — I would never ask to do something that's gonna put the lights out for you."Acknowledge the prospect's circumstances before checking financial capacity
26"Do you have a little bit of breathing room to be able to invest and set this up?"Confirm the prospect has capacity to invest
QUALIFICATION CLEARED AND EXPECTATIONS SET

Booking and homework

#Verbatim / instructionReason / purpose
27[BOOK]

"I'd love to book you a call so we can drop right into what we would do in your situation. You'll get a chance to see if it's a match for where you're heading."		Move the qualified prospect to the advisor call and frame it as a mutual fit check
28ASSIGN HOMEWORK: "I'm gonna send you a video, I'll need you to watch before we talk. It will take no more than 10 minutes."Assign brief preparation that builds context before the next call
29"Now, if you can't watch it, don't not show up. There's no grade. But I suggest you make time for it so you can get the most out of our time together."Reduce the risk of no-showing while still encouraging preparation
30"Can I have your word that if you can't make the call for any reason, you will just let me know?"Secure a communication commitment and protect the scheduled appointment
