Booking and homework

Verbatim / instructionReason / purpose
[BOOK]

"I'd love to book you a call so we can drop right into what we would do in your situation. You'll get a chance to see if it's a match for where you're heading."		Move the qualified prospect to the advisor call and frame it as a mutual fit check
ASSIGN HOMEWORK: "I'm gonna send you a video, I'll need you to watch before we talk. It will take no more than 10 minutes."Assign brief preparation that builds context before the next call
"Now, if you can't watch it, don't not show up. There's no grade. But I suggest you make time for it so you can get the most out of our time together."Reduce the risk of no-showing while still encouraging preparation
"Can I have your word that if you can't make the call for any reason, you will just let me know?"Secure a communication commitment and protect the scheduled appointment
