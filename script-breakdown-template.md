# Call Script Breakdown — Tim qualifying call

Reusable script breakdown. Verbatim lines are preserved from the supplied
Word document. Bracketed text is a prompt, condition, or internal instruction
rather than a line to read word-for-word.

**Formatting key:**

- <span style="color:#2563eb"><em>Blue italics</em></span> = something to say.
- <span style="color:#7c3aed"><strong>Purple bold</strong></span> = a question to ask the client.
- <span style="color:#db2777"><strong>Bold pink</strong></span> = an action or process instruction.
- <span style="color:#ea580c"><strong>Bold orange</strong></span> = a branch header or branch opener.
- 🟡 = an expectation being set with the prospect.
- 🟢 = a qualification question — information we need to gather.

## Opening and discovery

| | Reason / purpose |
| --- | --- |
| <span style="color:#2563eb"><em>"Hi <span style="color:#000000">[PROSPECT NAME]</span> I just saw that you booked a call. Wanted to see if you had a couple minutes to go over some questions."</em></span> | Open the call and confirm availability |
| 🟢 **WHY FUNDING:** <span style="color:#7c3aed"><strong>"So what is the biggest struggle in your business that has you reaching out for funding?"</strong></span> | Open discovery — identify the business problem and motivation |
| <span style="color:#db2777"><strong>[REFLECT BACK YOUR UNDERSTANDING / SUMMARY]</strong></span> | Demonstrate listening and confirm the problem before moving on |
| 🟢 **HOW MUCH:** <span style="color:#7c3aed"><strong>"How much funding are you looking to acquire?"</strong></span><br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style="color:#ea580c"><strong>[OPTION A: ABOVE 100K]</strong></span>: <span style="color:#2563eb"><em>"Fantastic."</em></span><br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;🟡 <span style="color:#ea580c"><strong>[OPTION B: BELOW 100K]</strong></span>: <span style="color:#2563eb"><em>"Part of my job is to tell you straight about expectations. We guarantee our clients achieve at least $100,000. It's usually more. That's the scale at this level of this funding done the right way."</em></span> <span style="color:#7c3aed"><strong>"Would you be able to use that effectively?"</strong></span> | Qualify the requested funding amount &nbsp;&nbsp;Set the $100K expectation |
| 🟢 **AGE OF BUSINESS:** <span style="color:#7c3aed"><strong>"How long have you had this business registered?"</strong></span> | Qualify business age |

## Education and qualification

|  | Reason / purpose |
| --- | --- |
| 🟡 <span style="color:#OOOOO"><strong>EDUCATE / DIFFERENTIATE:</strong></span><span style="color:#2563eb"><em>"So this is a no-documentation process for this level of funding. We won't need proof of revenue or anything, but we do look at your personal credit..."</em></span> | Explain the program category and set expectations about what is evaluated |
| 🟢 **OVER $10K UNSECURED / CREDIT HISTORY:** <span style="color:#7c3aed"><strong>"What kind of personal credit cards do you have?"</strong></span> | Assess available personal credit history and borrowing capacity |
| 🟢 **IDEAL TIMELINE:** <span style="color:#7c3aed"><strong>"Do you have a deadline for this funding?"</strong></span> | Identify urgency and compare it with a realistic process timeline |
| 🟡 <strong>REALISTIC TIMELINE:</strong> <span style="color:#2563eb"><em>"Given what I hear so far, it looks like a realistic timeline to get to funding is likely [REALISTIC TIMELINE]"</em></span><br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style="color:#ea580c"><strong>[BRANCH A: ALL CRITERIA MET]</strong></span>: the next 6 weeks<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style="color:#ea580c"><strong>[BRANCH B: MOST CRITERIA MET]</strong></span>: the next 3-4 months<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style="color:#ea580c"><strong>[BRANCH B2: FEW CRITERIA MET]</strong></span>: 6-7 months<br><br>🟡 <strong>REALISTIC TIMELINE CHECK:</strong> <span style="color:#7c3aed"><strong>Does that work for you?</strong></span> | Set a realistic timeline when the available information supports it<br>Confirm acceptance of the timeline |
| 🟢 **DEROGATORIES / LATE PAYS, ETC.:** <span style="color:#7c3aed"><strong>"Do you know on your credit report — is there any, and I have to ask everybody this — are there any late pays, any derogatories, anything else?"</strong></span> | Surface credit problems that could affect qualification |
| 🟢 **INQUIRIES:** <span style="color:#7c3aed"><strong>"And roughly, do you know how many inquiries you have on your credit?"</strong></span> | Reveal additional credit-file data and possible obstacles |
| 🟢 **OTHER FUNDING ATTEMPTS:** <span style="color:#7c3aed"><strong>"What else have you tried for the funding?"</strong></span> | Learn what the prospect has already attempted<br>Acknowledge market skepticism and explain why trust matters |

## Pre-objection handle

|  | Reason / purpose |
| --- | --- |
| 🟢 <span style="color:#000000"><strong>IF PREVIOUSLY BURNT:</strong></span> <span style="color:#db2777"><strong>[VALIDATE]</strong></span> <span style="color:#2563eb"><em>"And this is something we hear all the time — the funding space is saturated... Because of that, it's going to be very important for us to build trust."</em></span> | Acknowledge market skepticism and explain why trust matters |
| 🟢 <span style="color:#000000"><strong>TRUST:</strong></span> <span style="color:#2563eb"><em>"Let's suppose you felt like we were the perfect fit for your larger vision, everything was also a match on your end, so we invited you into the program, what would you want to know about us, to feel comfortable investing in yourself through our program?"</em></span> | Invite concerns before presenting the next step; surface the prospect's buying criteria |
| 🟡 <span style="color:#00000"><strong>LIQUIDITY: </strong></span><span style="color:#2563eb"><em>"One thing I do want to let you know is that this isn't something that happens for free, right?"</em></span><br><br><span style="color:#2563eb"><em>"I know .... <span style="color:#000000">[PROSPECT'S SPECIFIC CIRCUMSTANCE]</span> and I would never ask to do something that's gonna put the lights out for you."</em></span><br><br>🟢 <span style="color:#7c3aed"><strong>"Do you have a little bit of breathing room to be able to invest and set this up?"</strong></span> | Introduce the investment expectation, acknowledge the prospect's circumstances with empathy, then confirm capacity to invest |

## Booking and homework

|  | Reason / purpose |
| --- | --- |
| <span style="color:#db2777"><strong>[BOOK]</strong></span><br><br><span style="color:#2563eb"><em>"I'd love to book you a call so we can drop right into what we would do in your situation. You'll get a chance to see if it's a match for where you're heading."</em></span> | Move the qualified prospect to the advisor call and frame it as a mutual fit check |
| <span style="color:#db2777"><strong>ASSIGN HOMEWORK:</strong></span> <span style="color:#2563eb"><em>"I'm gonna send you a video, I'll need you to watch before we talk. It will take no more than 10 minutes."</em></span> | Assign brief preparation that builds context before the next call |
| <span style="color:#2563eb"><em>"Now, if you can't watch it, don't not show up. There's no grade. But I suggest you make time for it so you can get the most out of our time together."</em></span> | Reduce the risk of no-showing while still encouraging preparation |
| <span style="color:#7c3aed"><strong>"Can I have your word that if you can't make the call for any reason, you will just let me know?"</strong></span> | Secure a communication commitment and protect the scheduled appointment |

## 🟡 Expectations we need to set

- Funding scale 
- What gets evaluated 
- Realistic timeline 
- Liquidity 

## 🟢 Qualification information we need

- Purpose of funds
- Amount desired
- Timeline desired
- Age of business
- Unsecured credit age
- Unsecured credit limits
- Unsecured credit utilization
- Late pays, derogatories, or other credit red flags
- Number of nquiries
- Previous funding attempts
- Liquidity
